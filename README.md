# Catharage

## Overview

Catharage features built-in support for Archipelago. This readme assumes that you have at least a basic understanding of Catharage's progression and structure - if you're new to the game as a whole I recommend playing the tutorial as well as a couple of hours of regular gameplay to familiarize yourself with the mechanics.

## How to access Archipelago in-game

Archipelago options and functions can be found under the "3rd party" button in the Pause Menu. In there you can switch between regular and Archipelago-specific progression, reset that progression and connect to an Archipelago server. Note that connecting to an Archipelago server is only possible when using the Archipelago progression, and regular multiplayer is unavailable while Archipelago Progression is active.

## Items

Catharage offers the following "items" to Archipelago:
### Objectives
These are priority items required to beat the game. Getting an objective will unlock the corresponding objective POI for spawning in sectors, but will not credit the material and currency rewards that usually come from completing objectives - those still come from completing the objective "manually".
### Ships
All ships beyond the few unlocked at game start will need to be found via Archipelago. Ships found this way are unlocked directly without having to spend resources on them, and do not depend on their respective faction objectives.
### Upgrades
The five upgrades usually locked behind completion of a faction objective track are available as Archipelago items and immediately available regardless of objective progression.
### Filler
Filler items are Scrap, Credit and Material Drops, Item Drops, Fleet Member support, repairs and one Trap Item (an Ambush).

## Checks

Completing any regular objective gives a priority check corresponding to the objective completion status in the "Objectives" menu. Since actually achieving the objective is an item, doing this check will not move the objective track for the faction, and it is possible to "stack" objective completions by getting multiple objectives for one faction before you can complete them yourself. In this case, all outstanding checks will be completed simultaneously. This may sound somewhat confusing but will make sense once you're in-game ;)

In addition to these priority checks there are filler "World Checks". These can drop from any enemy that normally drops materials and can also replace world loot items, with number and chance configurable in the APWorld Options. World Checks are virtual items that are pulled from a list upon pickup, so it is not possible to miss them. They are also categorized into Tiers, with higher Tiers requiring more Objectives completed to access. This is so that critical early game items for other players don't land on World Check 499, holding up the run.

## Options

### World Loot Checks

Determines how many World Loot Checks to generate for the Archipelago. The more checks, the longer the Catharage player has to play. The default value of 50 makes for a relatively short Catharage playthrough, if playing with long games 200+ is recommended.

### World Loot Chance

Determines the chance for random world items and enemy material drops to become World Loot Checks. Lowering this chance significantly increases the playtime for the Catharage player, so it is recommended to set this to a high value, especially if combined with a high number of Checks.

## General Tips

Playing with Archipelago makes Catharage somewhat easier than a regular playthrough but can also be overwhelming, especially for newer players. Since most ship unlocks will come directly from other players and more high quality loot is available, moving through the progression is fast - on the other hand, since critical upgrades like armor regen and powerful ships are Archipelago-distributed, players may have to go long stretches without them. To have a smooth experience I recommend focusing on long-term runs that accumulate lots of power to have quick access to objectives once they become relevant, and to kill many enemies!
